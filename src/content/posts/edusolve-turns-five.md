EduSolve began in Kozhikode, Kerala, in 2021 with a simple idea: every child deserves a teacher who knows them by name. Five years on, we teach students across the Gulf one-on-one, online, and support them between classes with the EduSolve Learning App.

Today, as we celebrate our fifth anniversary, one thing I feel most grateful for is our incredible team.

## Five years, many challenges — never faced alone

Over the past five years, we have faced many challenges, problems, difficult situations and unexpected moments.

But every time we faced a problem, our team came together to solve it. That is our real strength.

Our journey was not always easy. But because of the dedication, commitment, trust and hard work of every team member, we kept moving forward.

## Built by a team, not one person

EduSolve is not built by one person. It is built by the collective efforts of an amazing team.

To every team member who has been part of this journey — thank you.

- Thank you for standing with EduSolve.
- Thank you for believing in our vision.
- Thank you for facing every challenge together.
- Thank you for turning problems into solutions.
- Thank you for being the strength behind our journey.

Our team is more than a group of employees. Our bond is what makes us stronger.

## Five years of learning and growth

Five years of challenges. Five years of learning. Five years of growth. And countless memories together.

**One Team. One Bond. One Vision.**

## And to our families

None of this would mean anything without the families who trust us with their children’s learning. Thank you for every demo class booked, every question asked and every milestone shared with us. This success belongs to every member of the EduSolve family — our team, our tutors, and you.

Here’s to the next five years.
